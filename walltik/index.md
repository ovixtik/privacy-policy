## English

### Introduction
**Walltik** is owned and operated by **OVIXTIK**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

### Data We Collect
- **Expense Data:** Stored locally on your device only and never sent to any server.
- **Categories & Budgets:** Stored locally.
- **Subscription Data:** Processed through Google Play Billing. We do not store any payment information.

### How We Use Data
- To track and record daily expenses
- To generate monthly expense reports
- To process subscriptions via Google Play

### Data Sharing
**We do not share your data with any third party.** All data is stored locally on your device only.

### Data Security
- All data is stored locally on your device
- We have no access to your personal data

### Your Rights
- Right to access your data
- Right to edit or delete any record
- Right to delete all data (by uninstalling the app)

### Children
This app is not directed at children under 13 years of age.
